Questions

Billing, answered.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Sign up with your mobile number, set up your shop and use the full Growth plan for 14 days. We only ask for payment if you choose a plan.

What happens when the trial ends?

The assistant pauses and your shop stays exactly as you left it: catalog, customers, settings. Choose a plan whenever you are ready and it switches back on.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and credit the unused part of your current period. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I pay, and do I get a GST invoice?

You get a GST invoice for every payment, available from the Billing screen. Payment is by UPI or bank transfer; online card payments are coming.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time; yearly plans are paid up front and give you two months free.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. The assistant works on your own business number, and your WhatsApp Business app keeps working next to it.
