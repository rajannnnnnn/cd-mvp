How it works

Set it up once. Then it sells while you work.

1

Tell it about your shop

Add your products, prices and opening hours. Set how low you’ll go on each item. Show it a few of your own replies so it writes like you.

2

It talks to your customers

It answers questions from your catalog, quotes prices, and negotiates in small steps, never below your lowest price. Replies come in short messages with a natural pause, not a wall of text.

3

You step in when it matters

Orders, complaints and questions it can’t answer reach your WhatsApp right away. Reply yourself and the assistant steps back so you never talk over each other.

You hold the price

The assistant can’t give away your margin.

For every item you decide whether to show the price, give a range, or keep it for yourself. If customers may bargain, you set the lowest price you’ll accept. That number is stored separately: the assistant never sees it, and prices come from a calculator, not from the AI’s imagination.

  • ✓Five ways to talk price: show it, “starts from”, a range, only after a couple of questions, or “I quote myself”.
  • ✓Discounts with conditions: only when they buy two, pay an advance, or are a returning customer.
  • ✓Below your lowest price? It brings you in instead of agreeing.

Honest by design

Your customers’ trust is your business.

A sales assistant that lies or pressures costs you customers. So ours is built not to.

  • 🚫No made-up urgency. It only says “only 3 left” when your stock count says so.
  • 🪪Honest if asked. If a customer sincerely asks whether it’s a person, it says it’s the shop’s assistant and offers to bring you in.
  • 🔒Never asks for card numbers, passwords or OTPs.
  • ✋Opt-outs respected. Customers who say stop are not messaged again. Personal contacts are never answered by the assistant.
  • 🏪Your data stays yours. One shop’s customers and prices are never used for another. You can have it deleted.
Questions

Things shop owners ask us

Will it give discounts I didn’t allow?

No. Every price it quotes comes from a calculator that follows your rules. Your lowest price is kept apart from the AI, so it can’t reveal or go below what it never sees. If a customer wants less, it brings you in.

What if it doesn’t know the answer?

It tells the customer you’ll get back to them and sends you the question. When you answer, it can remember the answer for next time, but only after you confirm.

Do I need a new phone number?

It’s built to run on your shop’s own WhatsApp Business number, so customers keep messaging the number they already know. During the pilot we connect it with you.

Can I still reply myself?

Always. The moment you reply to a customer, the assistant pauses on that chat for a while so you never talk over each other. You can also take over from the dashboard.

What does it cost?

We’re running pilots with a small group of shops first, and will set simple pricing once we’ve measured what it really costs to serve a conversation. Pilot shops hear about pricing before anything is charged.

Is my customers’ data safe?

Each shop’s data is separated from every other shop’s, enforced in the database itself. See our Privacy policy and how to delete your data.
